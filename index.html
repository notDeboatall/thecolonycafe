About us

The Colony Café is a small counter-service spot with a few stools and a menu that runs from tea to pizza. Our bestsellers include Cold Coffee, Café Specials, and the Paneer Tikki Burger. We are open daily from 10 am to 2 pm and 4 pm to 11 pm. Order at the counter, grab a seat, stay as long as you like. We opened in 2026.
